About

The PM who prototypes with AI.

For over a decade I've lived where atmospheric science meets the balance sheet — leading catastrophe modeling for global (re)insurers, then moving into AI product ownership to rebuild how that work gets done.

I led 300+ analysts across RMS, AIR, and Validus models in one of the industry's largest modeling operations. Then I went deeper: a master's in Catastrophe Modeling and Resilience, product leadership on Verisk's Touchstone platform, and now AI products on Palantir Foundry — plus TensorCat, my GPU-accelerated hurricane modeling engine, built to prove open models can outrun the incumbents.

Along the way I founded the Cat Modeling Network Association and IndiaCat — a professional home for the thousands of modelers powering the industry from India.
