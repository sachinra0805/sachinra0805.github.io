FAQ

Questions, answered

How hard are these tests, really?

Deliberately a notch above the real TOEFL (about C1+ level). If you can hold your own here, the real test should feel more comfortable — that's the point of practising above level.

How do I get an account?

Accounts are issued by your tutor — there's no public sign-up form. If you already have a Google account your tutor has approved, just use "Continue with Google" on the sign-in page.

Where do my scores go?

Every finished section is recorded to your account automatically and shown on your dashboard, with your skill averages and practice streak. Your tutor (and a linked parent) can see them too.
